====================================================
 LIEVORE HOME SERVICES – WordPress Theme Setup Guide
====================================================

WHAT YOU GET
------------
A complete, custom WordPress theme with 4 pages:
  - Home (front-page.php)
  - Services (page-services.php)
  - About (page-about.php)
  - Contact / Quote Form (page-contact.php)


STEP 1 – GET WORDPRESS HOSTING
--------------------------------
You'll need a web host that supports WordPress. Good affordable options:
  • Bluehost (bluehost.com) – ~$3/mo, includes free domain
  • SiteGround (siteground.com) – ~$4/mo, fast support
  • Hostinger (hostinger.com) – ~$2/mo, cheapest option
  • WP Engine (wpengine.com) – ~$25/mo, premium managed hosting

Most hosts have a "1-click WordPress install" in their control panel.
Install WordPress, then log into your site at: https://yoursite.com/wp-admin


STEP 2 – INSTALL THE THEME
----------------------------
1. Zip the entire "lievore-home-services-theme" folder
   (Right-click → Compress/Zip on Mac/Windows)

2. In your WordPress dashboard:
   Go to: Appearance → Themes → Add New → Upload Theme

3. Upload the zip file, then click "Activate"


STEP 3 – CREATE YOUR PAGES
----------------------------
In WordPress dashboard → Pages → Add New, create these 4 pages:

  Page Title  |  Template to Select (in Page Attributes panel)
  ------------|---------------------------------------------------
  Home        |  (no template needed — uses front-page.php)
  Services    |  Services Page
  About       |  About Page
  Contact     |  Contact Page

Then go to: Settings → Reading
  → Set "Your homepage displays" to "A static page"
  → Set "Homepage" to your "Home" page


STEP 4 – UPDATE YOUR CONTACT INFO
-----------------------------------
Go to: Appearance → Customize → Business Contact Info

Update these fields:
  • Phone Number  (e.g. (412) 555-1234)
  • Email Address (e.g. mike@lievore.com)
  • Service Area  (e.g. Pittsburgh & Surrounding Areas)
  • Business Hours


STEP 5 – SET YOUR NAVIGATION
------------------------------
Go to: Appearance → Menus → Create Menu

Add these pages: Home, Services, About, Contact
Set the menu location to "Primary Navigation"
Save.


STEP 6 – POINT YOUR DOMAIN
----------------------------
If you already own LievoreHomeServices.com:
  1. In your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)
     update the nameservers to point to your new host, OR
  2. Update the A record to your host's IP address.
  Your host's support team can walk you through this — just ask.


OPTIONAL – CONTACT FORM EMAIL
-------------------------------
The quote form sends emails using WordPress's built-in wp_mail().
To make sure emails actually deliver, install the free plugin:
  "WP Mail SMTP" → configure it with your Gmail or email account.
This ensures form submissions reach your inbox reliably.


QUESTIONS?
-----------
The theme is clean, standard WordPress code — any WordPress developer
or your hosting provider's support team can help with customizations.
